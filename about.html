About GarbageP2P

A peer-to-peer marketplace that puts fair scrap prices back in the hands of the people who collect it.

Our mission

Every day, thousands of waste collectors, street sweepers and manual sorters gather recyclable scrap — but they rarely get a fair price, because they sell through layers of middlemen.

GarbageP2P removes those layers. Collectors post what they've gathered, dealers and recyclers post what they need, and both sides negotiate directly. Every closed deal is published, so the whole market can see real prices.

More money for collectors, reliable supply for recyclers, and more waste diverted from landfills into recycling.

ℹ️ About this build

This is the static GitHub Pages edition of GarbageP2P. It runs entirely in your browser with no server — sample data is loaded from JSON, and anything you post, book or negotiate is saved to your browser's localStorage. A full-featured PHP/MySQL version (with real accounts, multi-user chat and an admin panel) also exists for server hosting.
